# Session Notes — 2026-04-19 (Part A)

## Changes

### 1. v3 Agentic Architecture — Design and Discovery Documents

**Feature request:** Post-mortem analysis of the April 18 2026 market event (BTC/ETH surge, zero entries despite 18 valid buy signals) revealed a coordination gap in Kryptos v2. Full agentic redesign commissioned.

**Root cause / Motivation:** Kryptos v2 operates as a single monolithic decision loop. The April 18 event exposed three failure modes: (1) HOLD-signal pairs suppressed from LLM context while still holding positions, (2) no cross-pair capital reallocation logic, (3) buy_min_score and caution_factor tuning accumulated to the point where no pair can enter during volatile surges. A multi-agent persona-based architecture was designed to address these.

**Implementation:** Eight design and discovery documents produced under `docs/v2-agentic/`:

- `problem-statement-crypto-Bot-Missed-Upswing.md` — Root cause analysis of the April 18 event; five failure hypotheses mapped to signal-scoring, prompt filtering, and risk-manager gates
- `Generic-multi-Agent-Design.md` — General multi-agent design pattern (Orchestrator + Specialist Agents + Tool Layer)
- `Persona-Based-Crypto-Bot-Design.md` — Kryptos v3 persona concept: Conservative / Medium / High risk archetypes mapped to buy_min_score, caution_factor, TP%, and max drawdown tolerance
- `BRD-v3.md` — Full Business Requirements Document v3.0 (supersedes `docs/business_requirements.md` v2.4); covers 75 functional requirements across 13 epics (E12–E24)
- `Architecture-Design-v3.md` — Technical architecture for v3: Orchestrator, QSA (Quantitative Signal Agent), AIE (AI Execution Agent), ROM (Risk & Operations Monitor), SHIELDA (Safety & Halt Layer), RAA (Research Analyst Agent), Audit Agent; pipe-format LLM payload; MCP integration
- `System-Interface-Changes-v3.md` — Delta between v2 and v3: new modules, modified interfaces, removed modules, database schema additions
- `Traceability-Matrix-v3.md` — Full FR-to-story traceability (75 FRs × 62 stories); every requirement traced to at least one user story
- `User-Stories-Sprint-Plan-v3.md` — 62 user stories across 11 sprints (S1–S11), with acceptance criteria, story points, squad assignments (Epic Index E12–E24)
