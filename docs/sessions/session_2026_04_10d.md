# Session Notes — 2026-04-10 (Part D)

## Changes

### 1. Reduce early-sell TP proximity guard from 80% to 60% (#138)

**Bug report:** The 80% proximity guard created an asymmetric trap. On a TP=12% pair, the LLM could not propose_sell until P&L ≥ +9.6%, but the SL fires at -5%. A trade reaching +7% with clear momentum reversal was forced to hold and risk a -5% stop — a 14.6% swing from peak to stop.

**Root cause:** The 80% threshold was too conservative, locking in positions well past the optimal exit window.

**Fix:**
- `config.yaml`: `early_sell_min_tp_proximity_pct` changed from `80` to `60`
- `.claude/skills/trading-rules/SKILL.md`: updated examples (≥12% on 20% TP, ≥7.2% on 12% TP)
- `tests/test_risk_manager.py`: renamed `test_sell_blocked_below_80_pct_of_tp` → `test_sell_blocked_below_60_pct_of_tp`; renamed `test_sell_allowed_at_85_pct_of_tp` → `test_sell_allowed_at_65_pct_of_tp`; updated boundary price from $110.20 to $107.80 (65% of 12% TP)
- `tests/test_per_pair_params.py`: updated config fixture from 80 to 60

**Effect:** Early sell now allowed at 60% of TP (e.g. +7.2% on 12% TP pair). Still prevents LLM panic-selling at +1–2% (min_profit_floor_pct=1% remains). The guard is already configurable via `trading.early_sell_min_tp_proximity_pct` in config.yaml — no code changes needed.
