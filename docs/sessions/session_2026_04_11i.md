## Changes

### 1. Fix: position gate uses remaining cash, not position count (#165)

**Bug report:** With 5 open positions (TRX $100, AVAX $100, JUP $70, SUI $35, TON $100 = $405
invested) and $594 cash remaining, all further buys were blocked. `max_open_positions: 5` fired
before any cash guard was evaluated.

**Root cause:** Two compounding problems:

1. `main.py` computed `max_per_trade = total_usd × max_position_pct`. When caution_factor shrinks
   individual positions (SUI 0.35×$200=$70→$35, JUP 0.5→$70), slots are consumed at fractional
   capital. The LLM was also being told its ceiling based on total portfolio ($200), not remaining
   cash ($119 with $594 available).

2. `validate_buy()` step 2 checked `open_positions_count >= self._max_open_positions` as a pure
   count gate — it fired and returned False **before** the cash reserve guards (step 3, guard 0.5)
   were ever reached. So $544 of deployable cash was permanently ignored once 5 slots filled.

**Fix — `main.py` line ~341:**
```python
# Before:
max_per_trade = total_usd * (config.get("trading", {}).get("max_position_pct", 30) / 100)

# After:
max_per_trade = cash_usd * (config.get("trading", {}).get("max_position_pct", 30) / 100)
```
LLM now sees a position ceiling proportional to available cash, not total portfolio.

**Fix — `src/risk/risk_manager.py` `validate_buy()` step 2:**

The count cap now only fires when BOTH conditions are true:
- `open_positions_count >= max_open_positions` (count ceiling hit)
- `deployable_cash < min_order_usd` (no cash for another trade anyway)

When count is at ceiling but cash is available, execution falls through to the existing cash guards
(step 3: min cash reserve check; guard 0.5: deployable below min_order_usd; step 5: tradable cash
cap). These guards already correctly prevent over-deployment.
